Welcome! I'm Angelo Barajas, a designer born and raised in the Los Angeles area. I got my B.A in Design from University of California, Davis where I mainly focused on UI/UX projects and also dabbled in graphic and motion design. This is a fine collection of those finished works, along with some of the process, made during my time. Also some of my photography is here, which is what inspired me to do design in the first place. Whether it be a brand outline, mock poster or website wirefrime, I make sure to have fun with all my designs becuase I believe that passion sees through design.
